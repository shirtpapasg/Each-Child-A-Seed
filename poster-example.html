Less food waste at home

USE ME FIRST!

How one box in our fridge helped my family waste less food.

1 The problem

We weighed our food waste for 7 days.

1.8 kg

in one week!
1.1 kg could have been eaten.

2

2 Why? The 5 Whys

🥬 The bayam turned yellow, so we threw it away.
WHY? We forgot it was there.
WHY? It was hidden at the back.
WHY? New groceries went in front.
WHY? Nobody checks what is old.
WHY? We have no place for “eat soon” food.
🔑 The real cause is a habit, and habits can change!
7

3 My solution: the Use Me First box

Food that must be eaten soon goes in a red box at eye level, where everyone sees it when they open the fridge.

Every food gets a colour sticker:

Green = fresh
Yellow = eat soon
Red = eat today!

Peels and bones cannot be eaten, so they go to compost, which gives nutrients back to the soil.

4 Did it work?

Food that could have been eaten, before and after the box:

Week 1
1100 g
Week 2
450 g

That is 650 g less, about 59% less food wasted!

🔧 Version 2: Mum said the box was too low, so she kept missing it. I moved it up to the middle shelf, at eye level.

🧪 Fair test: I weighed the food the same way both weeks. But we had a family dinner in week 1, so I will test for one more week to be sure.
