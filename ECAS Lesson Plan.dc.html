Primary 3 Science Life Cycles of Plants Every Child A Seed

Each Child a Seed

Teacher lesson plan · 4 periods × 30 min over 4 weeks · Classroom and canteen

Level
Primary 3
Time
4 × 30 min + check-ins
Plants
Thai Basil, Kang Kong, Bayam
Setting
Classroom + canteen

Learning outcomes

By the end of the unit, students will be able to:

  1. Observe the life cycles of plants grown from seeds over a period of time.
  2. Show curiosity by posing questions about the life cycle of plants.
  3. Show care and concern by being responsible towards plants.

These are the Every Child A Seed (NParks) outcomes. The unit also links to food security: all three plants are edible, and the display sits in the canteen where students eat.

The life cycle model used throughout
Seed→ germination → Young plant→ maturation → Adult plant→ pollination & fruiting → Seed

3 stages, 3 processes (NParks model). Always say “young plant”. If a student says “seedling”, accept it and restate it as “young plant”.

Prepare beforehand

  • One plant starter kit per student (pot, potting mix, seeds) and a printed Each Child a Seed journal (A5 booklet).
  • Newspaper, a spoon, a stick, a cup of water and a 15 cm ruler per student or pair.
  • A spare class pot of each plant, sown by the teacher on the same day, as a backup for any child whose seeds fail.
  • Devices (at least one per pair). Students sign in with class + register number + 4-digit PIN.
  • “I wonder…” board near the canteen display, with printed wonder cards. Care roster and pledge on display.
  • Test both NParks videos on the classroom screen: A City in Nature and Step-by-step Planting Guide.
  • Hand out the NParks My Plant Journal as a take-home keepsake. Students record in this journal only.
Plant assignment and plant buddies
  • Each child draws a seed packet in the app (Period 2). It feels random, but the app keeps the class balanced at about a third per plant.
  • The app forms plant buddy groups of 3–4 so that every group has all three plants. A group of four has one plant twice.
  • You can override any assignment or group from the teacher page.

Between periods · Observing

Daily · 10 seconds

“Has it sprouted?” The child or roster buddy taps 🌰 Not yet or 🌱 Yes.

On “Yes”, the app works out the days to sprout from the planting date and unlocks the I sprouted! page.

Weekly check-in · 7 min
  • Water if the soil feels dry.
  • Measure tallest and shortest young plant. Work out the difference.
  • One-tap photo (plants only, no faces). Waits for approval.
  • “How is my plant doing?” check.
  • Add an “I wonder…” card. Open a seed packet.

Common misconceptions

  • “Seeds need soil and sunlight to start growing.” They start with water, air and warmth, using stored food. Light matters once the young plant appears. (Sim 1, E1)
  • “More water is always better.” Roots need air too. Too much water drowns them. (Sim 1, K9)
  • “Trees don’t have stems.” A trunk is a big, woody stem. (Plant Parts Lab, rain tree)
  • “All plants make seeds.” Ferns are plants that don’t. (Seed Album “No seed!” cards)
  • “The first two leaves are normal leaves.” They are seed leaves. They look different and later drop off. (4 or more leaves! page)
  • “Vegetables aren’t fruits.” Chilli, tomato and cucumber are fruits because they hold seeds. (E2)

Explore Corner (optional, not assessed)

  • Seed Album. Random packets of 3 seeds. Students label each seed’s parts before it goes into the album. Duplicates become seed points, and 3 points buy any missing seed, so every child can finish. Includes “No seed!” fern cards.
  • E1 Open a Seed (P1) · E2 Fruit or Not? (P4) · E3 Pollinator Match (P3) · E4 Speed Race (P2–P3)
  • Try at home: DIY Bokashi compost (NParks video, with adult help), gardens to visit, grow it again at home.

Notes on the app

  • Sign-in: class + register number + PIN. No personal emails. A .json save file is available as a backup.
  • Guidance: a glowing ring shows students where to look and what to tap next. Turn it off from the teacher toggle when presenting.
  • Uploads: plants only, no faces. Up to 3 per week per child. Nothing appears in albums or on the canteen wall until you approve it.
  • Teacher page: approve photos, see each child’s plant status (🟢 🟡 🔴), milestones, and pre/post quiz results.
